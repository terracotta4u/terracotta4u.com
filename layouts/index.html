AI is a new medium.

Terracotta builds software that explores what becomes possible when intelligence is treated as more than a feature.

We believe AI should expand what people are capable of, not lock them into another application, subscription, or walled garden.

We’re starting with Golem.

Golem

One AI. Yours.

Golem is a personal AI agent that learns about you, works across the tools you use, and grows more capable over time.

Instead of talking to a different assistant in every app, Golem gives you one continuous agent that comes with you.

Get Golem View on GitHub

Your AI shouldn’t start over every time you open a new app.

Today, intelligence is scattered across software.

One assistant knows your conversations. Another helps you write code. Another lives inside your work tools. Each has its own context, capabilities, and version of who you are.

You are the only thing connecting them.

Golem takes a different approach.

Instead of putting an AI inside every application, Golem gives you one agent that can work across them.

One agent, wherever you need it.

Golem meets you where you already are.

Talk to it through the interfaces you use. Let it work with your files, tools, services, and workflows. Move from one environment to another without leaving your agent (and its understanding of you) behind.

It remembers.

Golem builds context over time, so every interaction doesn’t have to begin from zero.

It acts.

Golem can use tools and operate software, not just tell you what to do.

It goes with you.

Your agent isn’t tied to a single chat window, website, or application.

An AI that grows with you.

The Golem you start with doesn’t have to be the Golem you have a year from now.

Teach it how you like things done. Give it new skills. Connect it to new tools. Add entirely new capabilities.

Golem is designed to be malleable, adapting to the person using it rather than asking the person to adapt to the software.

As your needs change, Golem can change with them.

Make it yours.

There shouldn’t be one correct way to use a personal AI.

Extend Golem yourself, install extensions built by others, or have Golem help create what it needs.

Add new ways to communicate. Connect new services. Use different model providers. Teach it new workflows.

Golem is a foundation you can shape around your own digital life.

Yours, actually.

A personal AI should belong to the person using it.

Golem is open source and self-hostable. You can choose the models behind it, inspect how it works, extend it, and run it on infrastructure you control.

Your agent shouldn’t disappear because a company changes direction, shuts down a product, or decides which models you’re allowed to use.

Golem is built so you can keep it, and keep making it your own.
