Your AI is fragmented.

One assistant knows your conversations. Another helps you write code. Another lives inside your work tools.

Each has its own context, capabilities, and version of who you are.

You are the only thing connecting them.

Golem takes a different approach.

Instead of putting a new AI inside every application, Golem gives you one agent that works across them.

It carries its understanding of you from one place to the next—whether you’re talking, working, browsing, or using the tools you rely on every day.

The applications can change. Your agent stays the same.

It remembers. It acts. It goes with you.
