Golem

One AI. Yours.

Golem is a personal AI agent that learns about you, works across the tools you use, and grows more capable over time.

GitHub

One agent, not another app.

Most AI assistants live inside a product. You open the app, give it context, and leave that context behind when you move somewhere else.

Golem is the agent itself.

Meet it through different interfaces, connect it to different tools, and let it work across the software you already use.

The interface can change. Your agent stays the same.

It remembers. It acts. It goes with you.

Remembers

Golem carries forward what it learns about you, your projects, your preferences, and the way you work.

Every interaction doesn’t have to start from zero.

Acts

Golem can use tools, work with files, interact with software, and carry out tasks on your behalf.

It doesn’t just tell you what to do. It can help do it.

Goes with you

Your agent isn’t tied to a single chat window.

Talk to Golem through the interfaces you use and connect it to the services and workflows you already rely on.

Where you meet Golem can change. Golem stays the same.

Grows with you.

Golem is meant to become more useful the longer you use it.

Teach it your workflows, preferences, and ways of doing things with skills written in natural language.

Teach Golem how you research a company. How you organize a project. How you prepare a report. How you want a recurring task handled.

Your Golem becomes increasingly specific to you.

Give it new capabilities.

Extensions let Golem do things it couldn’t do before.

Connect new tools. Add communication channels. Support new model providers. Integrate the software that matters to you.

Use extensions built by others, write your own, or have Golem help create what you need.

Golem is built to be extended, not finished.

Use the intelligence you want.

Golem isn’t tied to a single model provider.

Choose the models that make sense for you and change them as the landscape changes. Use different models for different kinds of work.

Your agent should be more durable than the model powering it.

Yours, actually.

Golem is open source and self-hostable.

Run it on infrastructure you control. Inspect how it works. Modify it. Choose the models behind it. Extend it however you want.

A personal AI shouldn’t disappear because a company changes direction, shuts down a product, or decides what you’re allowed to do with it.

Golem is built so your agent can actually belong to you.
